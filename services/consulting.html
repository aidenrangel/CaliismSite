Consulting & Staffing

Sometimes the gap isn't hardware — it's strategy, policy, or skilled hands on site. Caliism provides IT consulting and staffing that aligns your infrastructure decisions with where the business is actually going.

Advice from people who do the work

Our consulting isn't slideware — it comes from teams that physically decommission data centers, sanitize drives by the thousand, and deploy enterprise hardware every week. When we advise on data destruction policy, asset lifecycle strategy, or decommissioning roadmaps, the recommendations have already survived contact with real projects.

Engagements range from a focused policy review — what should your media sanitization standard actually require, and what should the certificates capture? — to long-term lifecycle planning across deployment, refresh, and retirement.

When you need execution capacity rather than advice, our staffing arm places skilled, vetted technicians on your site for projects or ongoing operations.
